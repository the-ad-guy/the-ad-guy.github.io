One shared view
of the journey.

Learn from the people closest to the customer and establish the baseline needed to choose the right problem.

  1. Align milestones and owners.Agree what qualified exploration, build commitment, production, and subscriber value mean across teams.

By day 30

A journey map, a baseline of stage conversion and velocity, and a ranked set of constraints with evidence and owners.

Days 31–60 / Instrument & test

A focused experiment
at the point of friction.

Choose a small number of changes that address the observed bottleneck and can produce a useful decision.

  1. Repair the essential measurement gaps.Connect milestones and validate attribution so the team can trust the experiment’s readout.
  2. Run a focused pilot.Test a use-case page, Blueprint follow-up, build handoff, or onboarding step according to the diagnosed constraint.
  3. Agree the decision before launch.Set a primary metric, quality guardrails, an owner, and a review window. Use account-level evidence when volume is limited.
By day 60

A working measurement view and initial experiment evidence: what changed, who progressed, and what needs a closer look.

  1. Decide what to scale, refine, or stop.Assess stage progression alongside account quality, customer feedback, and downstream adoption.
