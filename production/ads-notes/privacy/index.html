Ads Notes · Chrome extension

Privacy policy

Ads Notes has no servers and no accounts. Everything it touches goes from your browser directly to the Google Sheet you chose. This page explains exactly what it accesses, why, and what it never does.

Effective October 21, 2026. Applies to the Ads Notes Chrome extension, item ID nindafjblmbdjhmjkjcmfklcddkkjegj.

Back to Ads Notes

Who operates Ads Notes

Ads Notes is built and maintained by Tim Gibson, an individual developer, reachable at timjgib@gmail.com. There is no company behind it, no analytics vendor, no data processor, and no third party with access to anything the extension handles.

The short version

  • Ads Notes writes note rows into one Google Sheet that you choose by pasting its URL.
  • Those writes go straight from your browser to the Google Sheets API. They do not pass through any server operated by me.
  • I never receive, see, collect, or store your Google account data, your spreadsheet contents, or your advertising data.
  • Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with anyone, and nothing is used for advertising or to train any model.
  • You can revoke the extension’s access at any time, from inside the extension or from your Google account settings.

Google user data Ads Notes accesses

The spreadsheet you designate

When you paste a Google Sheets URL into the extension, Ads Notes reads that spreadsheet’s header row to work out which column holds which field, checks that the account you signed in with can edit it, and appends rows as you log notes. It reads a limited range of recent rows to avoid writing the same note twice. It does not list, search, open, modify, or delete any other file in your Google Drive.

Your Google account email address

Ads Notes reads the email address of the Google account you connect. It is written into the User column of your spreadsheet so that teams sharing one sheet can tell who logged which note. It is not transmitted anywhere else.

Page content on advertising platforms

On supported advertising platform pages, Ads Notes reads information already displayed on screen, such as the advertising account name and identifier, campaign name, ad group name, and the text of a note you save. On Google Ads it also observes the extension’s own capture of notes you create. This reading happens entirely inside your browser. The extension does not run on pages outside the advertising platforms listed in its permissions.

Settings you enter

Your spreadsheet URL and your preferences are stored by Chrome on your own computer using the extension storage API. If you have Chrome profile sync turned on, Chrome may sync those settings between your own devices under Google’s terms. I have no access to that storage.

Google API permissions requested

Ads Notes asks for the narrowest permissions that allow it to work. You see and approve these on Google’s own consent screen before anything is accessed.

  • See, edit, create, and delete all your Google Sheets spreadsheets (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets). Ads Notes needs write access to a spreadsheet that already exists and that you identify by URL. Google does not offer a narrower permission that can reach an existing spreadsheet chosen this way, so this is the minimum workable scope. In practice Ads Notes only ever reads from and appends to the single spreadsheet you configure.
  • See your primary Google Account email address (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email). Used solely to fill the User column of your spreadsheet.

How the information is used

Every piece of information described above is used for one purpose: assembling a note row and appending it to the spreadsheet you chose. There is no secondary use. Specifically, Ads Notes does not use your data for advertising, personalization, profiling, credit assessment, lending, analytics about you, market research, or training or improving any machine learning or artificial intelligence model, whether mine or anyone else’s.

Where the information goes

Ads Notes makes network requests to Google only, specifically to Google’s authentication endpoints and the Google Sheets API. The data in those requests is your note and the spreadsheet it belongs in. I operate no backend, no database, no logging service, and no analytics in the extension, so there is nowhere for your data to arrive even if I wanted it.

This website, theadguy.org, uses Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics for ordinary site traffic measurement. That applies to visitors reading this page, and is entirely separate from the extension, which contains no analytics of any kind.

Sharing and disclosure

Ads Notes does not transfer your information to any third party. It is not sold, rented, licensed, or shared. The only parties who can see your notes are Google, which hosts the spreadsheet, and the people you have personally shared that spreadsheet with.

Limited Use disclosure

Ads Notes’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

I retain nothing, because I receive nothing. The note rows live in your spreadsheet, under your control, and you can edit or delete them there at any time. The extension’s settings live on your computer and are removed when you uninstall the extension or clear its storage from the Chrome extensions page.

Withdrawing access

You can disconnect at any time using the Disconnect button in the extension, which revokes the token and clears the stored credentials. You can also revoke access independently at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage. Neither action deletes rows already written to your spreadsheet, since those are your records.

Security

Authentication uses Chrome’s built in identity service, which holds the access token in the browser and refreshes it as needed. The extension never sees or stores a refresh token, and there is no client secret embedded in it. All communication with Google uses HTTPS. The extension contains no remotely hosted code, as required by Chrome Manifest V3.

Children

Ads Notes is a professional tool for people managing advertising accounts. It is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If the extension’s data handling changes, this page is updated and the effective date at the top changes with it. Material changes that widen what Ads Notes accesses will require your consent through a new Google permission prompt before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data, go to timjgib@gmail.com. I answer personally.
